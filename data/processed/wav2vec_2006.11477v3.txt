Abstract

audioalonefollowedbyfine-tuningontranscribedspeechcanoutperformthebest
semi-supervisedmethodswhilebeingconceptuallysimpler. wav2vec2.0masks

test sets. When lowering the amount of labeled data to one hour, wav2vec 2.0

timeslesslabeleddata. Usingjusttenminutesoflabeleddataandpre-training
on53khoursofunlabeleddatastillachieves4.8/8.2WER.Thisdemonstratesthe
feasibilityofspeechrecognitionwithlimitedamountsoflabeleddata.1
